Privacy policy

Antigravity Mobile · Version 1.0

Antigravity Mobile is an independent third-party client. This policy explains the app's local features and its connection to Google's service.

Data kept on your device

Saved connection addresses, names, history and pin settings stay in this device's Keychain. The app does not enable iCloud Keychain synchronization for these records. Language preferences and website sign-in data are kept in the app's local storage. The app does not operate a developer account, conversation-relay or analytics server.

Google service data

Connecting, and reopening a saved connection, sends requests to Google's official website. Google processes account information, content and attachments you provide, service interactions, identifiers and technical information such as browser/device information, IP addresses and diagnostics under its own policies. Google may derive approximate location from technical information. Processing and retention depend on your account, settings and Google's terms.

The developer does not receive your Google passwords, cookies, tokens, conversations, code or workspace files from this app. The app does not extract or copy website credentials. Google's own website handles authentication and any required consent or verification.

Camera, photos and paste

The native QR scanner uses the camera only when you start scanning. QR recognition and reading a picture you select happen on your device; those scanner images are not uploaded by the native app. Pasting is initiated by you. Content or attachments you deliberately submit through Google's page are sent to Google as part of that service.

Advertising and tracking

The native app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and does not request advertising identifiers or device location. This does not mean Google's website processes no data; its practices are described in the linked policies.

Managing your data

You can restore or delete saved addresses in Settings. Deleting address history does not stop a session on your computer or sign you out of Google. Sign out and manage Google account/service data through Google's own controls. Uninstalling can remove app-container website data, while iOS can retain Keychain records.

Contact and service policies

Privacy questions: 1459636830@qq.com. Do not send passwords or private connection links.

隐私政策

Antigravity Mobile · 版本 1.0

Antigravity Mobile 是独立第三方客户端。本政策说明原生本机功能和连接 Google 服务时的数据处理。

保存在本机的数据

连接地址、名称、历史记录和置顶保存在仅本设备使用的钥匙串中，不启用 iCloud 钥匙串同步。语言偏好与网站登录数据保存在 App 本机存储中。开发者不运营自建账号、聊天中转或数据分析服务器。

Google 服务的数据

首次连接及自动恢复保存的连接时，会向 Google 官方网站发起请求。Google 根据自身政策处理账号信息、你提交的内容及附件、服务交互、标识符，以及浏览器/设备信息、IP 地址、诊断等技术信息，也可能据技术信息推断大致位置。具体用途和保留时间取决于账号、设置与 Google 条款。

开发者不会通过本 App 接收你的 Google 密码、Cookie、令牌、聊天、代码或工作区文件。App 不提取或复制网站凭据。登录、授权与必要的验证由 Google 页面处理。

相机、照片与粘贴

原生扫码仅在你主动操作时使用相机，二维码识别和你选择的图片在本机处理，原生 App 不上传这些扫码图片。粘贴由你主动发起。你在 Google 页面主动提交的内容或附件，会按该服务的功能发送给 Google。

广告与追踪

原生 App 不含广告或第三方分析 SDK，不请求广告标识符或设备定位。这不代表 Google 网站不处理数据；其数据规则见下方政策。

管理数据

可在设置中恢复或删除地址记录。删除地址不会停止电脑端会话或退出 Google 登录。退出账号和管理 Google 服务数据，请使用 Google 自身的设置。卸载可能删除 App 容器中的网站数据，但 iOS 可能保留钥匙串记录。

联系与服务政策

隐私问题请联系 1459636830@qq.com，请勿发送密码或私密连接。
