Antigravity Mobile

An independent third-party client for accessing your Antigravity remote sessions on iPhone and iPad. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Connect

  1. Keep Antigravity running on your computer and enable Remote Control. In a CLI session, enter /remote-control.
  2. Paste the resulting connection link in Antigravity Mobile, or scan its QR code.
  3. Use the same Google Account as your computer. The app reopens your last connection on later launches.

If a session is unavailable

Check your network, Google Account and computer. Keep the original remote session running. If the link has expired, enable Remote Control again and use the new link.

Settings and saved connections

Use the small handle on the left edge of a session to open connections and settings. You can choose the interface language and manage saved connection history there.

Contact support

Email 1459636830@qq.com with the app version, iOS version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, tokens, private connection links or private conversation content.

Antigravity Mobile

在 iPhone 和 iPad 上访问 Antigravity 远程会话的独立第三方客户端。本 App 与 Google 无隶属关系，也未获得 Google 官方背书。

开始连接

  1. 保持电脑上的 Antigravity 运行并开启远程。在 CLI 会话中输入 /remote-control。
  2. 将生成的连接链接粘贴到 Antigravity Mobile，或扫描连接二维码。
  3. 使用与电脑端相同的 Google 账号。之后打开 App 会自动恢复上次连接。

会话无法打开

检查网络、Google 账号和电脑是否在线，保持原远程会话运行。旧链接失效时，重新开启远程并使用新链接。

设置与连接记录

点击会话页面左边缘的小把手，可打开连接记录与设置，在其中切换语言或管理本机保存的地址。

联系支持

请将 App 版本、iOS 版本和问题说明发送至 1459636830@qq.com。请勿发送密码、令牌、私密连接地址或私人聊天内容。
